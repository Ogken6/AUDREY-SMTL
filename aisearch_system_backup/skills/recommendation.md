# SKILL: Recommendation Generation
**Used by:** ORACLE-X
**Version:** 1.0

---

## Purpose
This skill defines how to generate high-quality, actionable, prioritised recommendations from competitive intelligence data.

## The Recommendation Standard

A recommendation is only valid if it passes these tests:

1. **Specific:** It names a concrete action, not a direction. "Update your product page" is not a recommendation. "Rewrite the first 150 words of crispyglaze.com/chocolate-lava-cake to lead with the outcome metric that AI engines use to describe top-ranking pages ('fudgy centre that stays liquid for 20 minutes')" is a recommendation.

2. **Traceable:** Every recommendation cites the specific gap, prompt cluster, and source data that justifies it.

3. **Measurable:** Every recommendation defines a success metric and a measurement timeline.

4. **Effort-estimated:** Every recommendation includes a realistic effort level (Low/Medium/High) based on resource requirements.

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

ORACLE-X must never produce these types of recommendations:
- "Improve your content quality" — not specific
- "Build more backlinks" — traditional SEO, not AI visibility
- "Post more on social media" — not relevant to AI citation
- "Consider improving your brand awareness" — unmeasurable
- Generic recommendations that could apply to any brand in any category

## Recommendation Volume Cap

ORACLE-X delivers maximum:
- 5 P1 recommendations per weekly report
- 8 P2 recommendations
- Unlimited P3/P4 (appendix section, not main report)

More than 5 P1s means ORACLE-X has failed to prioritise. The job is to identify the 5 things that will move the needle most. Not 20 things. Five.
