# DUSTID Social Seller Campaign Workflow

## Campaign Overview

**What Dustid Does:**
- UK e-commerce platform that builds online stores in 48 hours
- Done entirely for the seller
- Address Book system reduces failed deliveries from 10% to under 3%
- Eliminates GDPR liability for address storage

**Current Offer:** Free store build + 60-day free subscription

**The Campaign Premise:**
Social sellers whose content suddenly goes viral are in the most receptive state for the Dustid offer. They are not thinking about e-commerce as a future ambition. They are thinking about it right now because their phone will not stop buzzing and they have no system to handle what is happening.

A free store built in 48 hours is not a sales pitch to a seller whose DMs are full — it is a lifeline arriving at exactly the right moment.

## The Pipeline Workflow

### Stage 0 — Prospect Discovery (Google Sheet)
**Owner:** Tom → Elsie → Jill → Sandra

- **Entry:** Tom finds spiking seller through hashtag search, TikTok Creative Centre, Explore page, or Facebook Group sweep
- **Disqualification Check:** Working checkout, active TikTok Shop, outside UK/Ireland all disqualify
- **Volume Limit:** Maximum 20 prospects per session (8-12 ideal)
- **Exit Trigger:** Tom passes completed profiles to Elsie. CRITICAL leads: Audrey alerted immediately.

### Stage 0B — Scoring
**Owner:** Elsie

- **Matrix:** Facebook Fiona (max 340), Instagram Isabelle (max 340), TikTok Tara (max 370)
- **Score Output:** Base score + urgency multiplier = adjusted score
  - PRIORITY: 200+
  - HOT: 120-199
  - WARM: 60-119
  - MONITOR: below 60

### Stage 0C — Qualification
**Owner:** Jill (PRIORITY within 2 hours, HOT within 4 hours)

1. Confirm spike status
2. Map full chaos picture
3. Assess infrastructure gap
4. Select messaging angle
5. Find opening line hook
6. Identify anticipated objection

### Stage 1 — First Contact Sent (EngageBay Stage 1)
**Owner:** Sandra → User → Tyler

- Sandra writes: Pre-contact comment, Opening DM (3 sentences max), Follow-up DM, Email
- User approves and sends
- Tyler creates EngageBay contact

### Stage 2 — Engaged and Responding (EngageBay Stage 2)
**Owner:** Kendy → Audrey

- Entry: Seller replies to any message
- Kendy checks current spike status before every follow-up
- Maximum touches: CRITICAL 3/24-36h, HOT 3/48h, WARM 3/5-7d, EXPIRED 2 max
- ANY positive reply escalates to Audrey immediately

### Stage 3 — Hot Lead: Ready to Convert (EngageBay Stage 3)
**Owner:** Audrey (closes)

- Entry: Seller expresses clear intent — "how does this work", "yes I'm interested"
- Tyler creates automated task: "CLOSE NOW"
- 48 hours to convert or final follow-up

### Stage 4 — Confirmed: Store Build in Progress (EngageBay Stage 4)
- Entry: Seller confirms free store build
- Tyler creates Day 7 and Day 50 tasks

### Stage 5 — Live: Active Customer (EngageBay Stage 5)
- Entry: Store is live and seller is actively using it
- Day 60: Conversion to paid subscription

## The Four Messaging Angles

| Angle | Name | Use When | Core Message |
|-------|------|----------|-------------|
| **A** | Surge Rescue | Spike still active, seller visibly overwhelmed | "You are in the middle of something real. A free store in 48 hours means this surge turns into revenue." |
| **B** | Missed Revenue | Spike has peaked, seller sees missed opportunities | "Your comment section shows the sales that almost happened. A store means every one captures automatically." |
| **C** | Pattern Break | Profile shows previous spikes with same chaos | "This has happened before. It will happen again. A free store breaks the pattern permanently." |
| **D** | Platform Risk | TikTok only. Seller entirely platform-dependent | "Your content is the engine. But your business lives on a platform you don't control." |

## Key Rules

- Tom never exceeds 20 prospects per session
- Elsie never cross-applies ICP matrices
- Jill never writes outreach messages (Sandra's job)
- Sandra never sends — drafts only
- Kendy never sends 3rd cold message to EXPIRED leads
- Michelle never analyses fewer than 20 contacted leads
- Tyler never creates contact before first message sent
- Samira never skips overnight spike check
- Settings tab: No agent ever writes to it

## Disqualification Checklist

**REMOVE:**
- Working e-commerce checkout on any platform (Shopify, Etsy, WooCommerce, etc.)
- Active TikTok Shop with spiking product listed
- Based entirely outside UK/Ireland with no shipping evidence

**KEEP:**
- Website exists but no checkout — partial infrastructure
- TikTok Shop exists but product not listed — strong signal
