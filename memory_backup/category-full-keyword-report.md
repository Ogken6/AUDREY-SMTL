## KEY INSIGHTS

### Why Your Exact Names Won't Rank
1. **Long-tail problem:** People search individual words like "dessert" or "brunch" — not full category names
2. **Search intent mismatch:** "Comfort Beverages" is not a common US search term
3. **SEO vs. category labels:** Website category labels ≠ search engine queries

### Best Strategy for Each Category
- **Sweet Treats** — Target: "easy dessert recipes", "homemade sweet treats"
- **Breakfast Ideas** — Target: "quick breakfast ideas", "easy breakfast recipes"  
- **Cozy Drinks** — Target: "hot chocolate recipe", "cozy drink recipes"
