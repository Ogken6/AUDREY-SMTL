## Additional Niches Recommended

### 1. Pet Products 🐕
**Why:** Huge UK market on social media. Small sellers selling pet treats, toys, accessories, and grooming products. They take orders via DM and struggle with address collection for shipping.

**Pain Points Dustid Solves:**
- Customers DM addresses for shipping - often incomplete/wrong
- No GDPR-compliant storage of customer data
- Viral pet content leads to order surges they can't handle

**Target:** UK pet product TikTok creators, Instagram pet shops

---

### 2. Jewelry 💎
**Why:** High-margin handmade jewelry is popular on Instagram and TikTok. Many solo sellers using DM for orders.

**Pain Points Dustid Solves:**
- Custom jewelry requires precise shipping addresses
- Repeat customers need saved addresses for reorders
- Professional checkout builds credibility for wholesale

**Target:** UK handmade jewelry Instagram sellers, TikTok jewelry creators

---

### 3. Home Decor 🏠
**Why:** Wall art, prints, decorative cushions, and home accessories do well on visual platforms. Growing niche post-COVID.

**Pain Points Dustid Solves:**
- Large items need accurate shipping addresses
- Customers often want tracked delivery
- GDPR concerns with storing customer addresses

**Target:** UK home decor Instagram shops, TikTok interior content creators

---

### 4. Print-on-Demand 📦
**Why:** Custom mugs, t-shirts, phone cases, canvases. Low barrier to entry, many small UK sellers.

**Pain Points Dustid Solves:**
- POD fulfilled by third party - addresses critical
- Order management through DMs is chaotic
- Fast checkout reduces cart abandonment

**Target:** UK POD sellers on Instagram, TikTok craft creators

---

### 5. Baby & Kids 👶
**Why:** Parents selling children's clothes, toys, accessories. Very active on Instagram. Data-conscious demographic.

**Pain Points Dustid Solves:**
- Parents concerned about data privacy (GDPR)
- Children's products often shipped to gifts - addresses must be accurate
- Repeat purchases common as kids grow

**Target:** UK baby/children's product Instagram shops

---

### 6. Vintage & Retro 👗
**Why:** Popular UK niche - vintage clothing, retro home items, antique finds. Strong community on Instagram and TikTok.

**Pain Points Dustid Solves:**
- Unique items often one-of-a-kind - must get right first time
- International shipping common - accurate addresses critical
- Professional checkout for higher price points

**Target:** UK vintage sellers Instagram, TikTok vintage curators

---

### 7. Garden & Plants 🌱
**Why:** Plants and garden decor exploded post-COVID. Many small UK growers selling via social media.

**Pain Points Dustid Solves:**
- Live plants need precise delivery timing
- Large plants require accurate shipping addresses
- Seasonal peaks (spring) cause order surges

**Target:** UK plant sellers Instagram, TikTok plant influencers

---

### 8. Stationery 📓
**Why:** Bullet journaling and stationery community is huge in UK. Notebooks, planners, stickers, washi tape.

**Pain Points Dustid Solves:**
- Repeat customers (journaling supplies)
- International orders common
- Small business owners want proper checkout

**Target:** UK stationery Instagram shops, TikTok craft supply creators

## Prospect Finding Notes

The Xpoz tool is better suited for finding influencers and business content rather than specific small product sellers. For actual prospect finding, recommended approaches:

1. **Manual Hashtag Research**: Search Instagram/TikTok hashtags like #ukhandmade, #britishmaker, #smallbusinessuk
2. **Facebook Groups**: UK small business groups have active sellers
3. **TikTok Shop**: Browse UK TikTok Shop sellers in target niches
4. **Etsy Sellers**: Many Etsy sellers also sell on social media - cross-reference

Target: 10-15 prospects per platform would require ~2-3 hours of manual research using browser tools.
