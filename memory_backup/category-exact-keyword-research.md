## Why Your Exact Names Don't Work

1. **Long-tail over individual words** — People search "dessert" or "easy dessert recipe", not "Desserts and Sweet Baked Goods"
2. **"Comfort Beverages" is not a common search term** — The US market searches "cozy drinks," "hot chocolate," "warm drinks"
3. **Category names vs. search intent** — SEO category names are different from what people type in Google
