# CONTENT GUIDELINES — How Content Gets Made

All content follows this pipeline. No exceptions.

---

## Pipeline Stages

### Stage 1: Marcus — Gap Analysis
Input: Website + niche
Output: Gap report (what's missing, what's needed)

### Stage 2: Elena — Humanize
Input: Topic/research notes
Output: Human-written draft (not AI-sounding)

### Stage 3: Raj — SEO Optimize
Input: Human draft
Output: SEO-ready draft

### Stage 4: Livy — Edit
Input: SEO draft
Output: Publish-ready or Feedback

### Stage 5: Ken — Publish
Input: Ready draft
Output: Published to WordPress

---

## Quality Standards

### AdSense Compliance
- Original content (not scraped)
- Helpful, not thin
- No policy violations
- Proper attribution when needed

### Human Voice
- Natural sentences
- Personal experience references
- Varied structure
- No obvious AI patterns

### SEO Requirements
- Target keyword in title
- Headers optimized
- Internal links
- Proper length (1000+ words for recipes)

---

## What Gets Rejected

- AI-sounding content
- Thin content (<500 words)
- Duplicate content
- Keyword stuffing
- Poor readability

---

*Every piece passes all stages.*
