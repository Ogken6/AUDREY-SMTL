# Spiced Orange Peel & Clove Comfort Brew — Cozy Warm Drinks Recipe

*This came into my life on a gray January afternoon when winter felt endless and I was about to toss orange peels into the compost. Now it's my winter survival tool — the thing I make when I need my kitchen to smell like happiness.*

**Prep Time:** 20 mins | **Servings:** 4 | **Keywords:** cozy drinks, warm drinks, orange tea, spiced drink, mulled drink

---

## Why This Recipe Works

**Orange peels** contain essential oils way more aromatic than the juice itself. When you simmer them, those oils release into the water — creating a fragrant, slightly bitter, beautifully complex citrus base.

**Cloves** transform plain orange tea into something warming. Whole cloves (not ground) give you that aromatic warmth without muddy grit.

**Brown sugar** brings molasses richness that white sugar simply can't replicate. It rounds everything out.

This brew is complex enough to be interesting but comforting enough for daily ritual.

---

## Ingredients

- 4 cups water
- Peels from 4 oranges (no excessive white pith)
- 8-10 whole cloves
- 1-2 star anise pods
- ¼ cup brown sugar (light or dark)
- Optional: 6-8 thin ginger slices

---

## Directions

1. **Prepare peels.** Tear or cut into 1-2 inch pieces. Smaller = more surface area = more flavor.

2. **Simmer.** Add water, peels, cloves, star anise, and brown sugar to a medium saucepan. Bring to boil over medium-high, then reduce to low.

3. **Gentle simmer.** Cook 15-20 minutes. Don't boil aggressively — you want gentle extraction, not a citrus storm.

4. **Steep.** Remove from heat, let steep 2-3 minutes more.

5. **Strain.** Fine-mesh strainer into a pitcher. Press gently on solids — don't squeeze or it'll get bitter.

6. **Serve.** Hot immediately, or cool and refrigerate. Reheat individual servings or serve over ice.

7. **Store.** Refrigerated up to 5 days, frozen (ice cube trays) up to 3 months.

---

## Chef's Tips

- **Whole cloves only** — ground cloves make it gritty. Less is more with these potent spices.
- **Tear the peels** — more surface area = more essential oils released.
- **Gentle simmer** — aggressive boiling makes it cloudy.
- **Strain without pressing** — the white pith underneath the peel is bitter.

---

## Variations

- **Mulled wine version:** Add 2 cups red wine to 2 cups strained brew. Heat gently (don't boil).
- **Apple cider version:** Replace half water with apple cider.
- **Lemon + orange combo:** Use both citrus peels for more complex flavor.
- **Ginger-heavy:** Triple the ginger for throat-soothing warmth.
- **Honey sweetened:** Use honey instead of brown sugar (add after cooking to preserve enzymes).

---

## Troubleshooting

- **Cloudy brew?** Simmer harder or pressed too hard while straining. Use cheesecloth for crystal clear.
- **Bitter?** Too much pith or simmered too long. Reduce time to 12-15 min.
- **Weak flavor?** Need more peels or longer simmer. Don't be shy.
- **Too spiced?** Too many cloves/star anise. Start smaller, add more next time.
