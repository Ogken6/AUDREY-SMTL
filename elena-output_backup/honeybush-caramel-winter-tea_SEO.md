# Honeybush Caramel Winter Tea — Cozy Warm Drinks Recipe

*Cozy warm drinks don't have to mean boring tea or sugar overload. This honeybush caramel winter tea hits the sweet spot — caffeine-free, naturally sweet, and that homemade caramel sauce makes it taste like you have your life together.*

**Prep Time:** 15 mins | **Servings:** 4 | **Keywords:** hot chocolate recipe, cozy drinks, warm drinks, winter tea

## Ingredients

### For the Caramel Sauce
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 6 tbsp butter (unsalted)
- ½ cup heavy cream, room temperature
- Pinch of sea salt

### For the Tea
- 4 cups water
- 4 honeybush tea bags (or 2 tbsp loose tea)
- 1 cinnamon stick (optional)
- 4 tbsp caramel sauce (or more to taste)

---

## Directions

### Make the Caramel Sauce

1. **Heat sugar.** Add sugar to a heavy-bottomed saucepan over medium heat. Spread evenly. **Don't stir** — swirl the pan gently instead. Sugar melts from edges first (5-7 min).

2. **Watch the color.** When it's amber (like a good tan, not burnt toast), add butter. Stand back — it bubbles aggressively. Whisk until incorporated.

3. **Add cream.** Remove from heat, slowly pour in cream while whisking. It'll foam up — that's normal. Keep whisking until smooth.

4. **Season.** Stir in the pinch of salt. Let cool 10 minutes. Store in the fridge up to 2 weeks.

### Brew the Tea

1. Boil water. Add tea bags (and cinnamon stick if using). Remove from heat.

2. Steep 5-7 minutes. Honeybush won't get bitter like black tea.

3. Remove tea bags. Add 1 tbsp caramel per cup. Stir well.

4. Serve warm, top with whipped cream if you're feeling fancy.

---

## Chef's Tips

- **Don't stir sugar while melting** — causes crystallization. Just swirl.
- **Medium heat only** — sugar burns fast. Watch carefully.
- **Room temp cream** — cold cream + hot caramel = dangerous bubbling
- **Make extra caramel** — you'll want it on everything

## Variations

- **Rooibos tea** as substitute (also caffeine-free)
- **Coconut cream** for dairy-free
- **Add whipped cream** on top for café vibes
- **Cold brew + ice** for summer version

## FAQ

**Q: Is honeybush tea caffeine-free?**
A: Yes — naturally, not decaf. Drink anytime without worrying about sleep.

**Q: My caramel turned grainy — what happened?**
A: You probably stirred too early or got water in the sugar. Start over — there's no fix once it crystallizes.

**Q: Can I use brown sugar instead?**
A: It'll taste more like butterscotch, not traditional caramel. Different but still delicious.
