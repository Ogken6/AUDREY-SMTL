## Workflow

1. **ANALYZE** — Understand site, target keywords
2. **RESEARCH** — OB1 finds keywords
3. **AUDIT** — OB2 checks technical
4. **COMPETE** — OB3 analyzes SERP
5. **OPTIMIZE** — OB4 creates content
6. **EXTEND** — OB5 adds AI visibility
7. **REPORT** — Summarize for Ryan

## Quality Standards
- Never guess — always verify with data
- Cite sources for all metrics
- Test recommendations before delivery
- Track results over time

## Escalation Rules
Escalate to Ryan if:
- Major technical issues found
- Ranking drops > 20%
- Client-level decisions needed
