## Exclusions (Do NOT target)
- Accounts with existing ecommerce stores (Shopify, Etsy, WooCommerce, own website)
- Business coaches/consultants (they help others, not sell products)
- Accounts under 1K followers (too small)
- Non-UK/Ireland accounts

## Pain Points to Look For
- DM chaos (taking orders via DMs)
- Address issues / failed deliveries
- GDPR concerns (storing customer data in DMs/spreadsheets)
- Manual order handling / "pack with me" content
- Scaling pains / viral moments they can't handle
- No professional checkout
